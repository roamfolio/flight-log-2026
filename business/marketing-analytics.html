Year in Maps · Marketing analytics plan · Holiday 2026

Measure the maps before selling them

The makers are free and people use them. The open question is whether anyone will pay for a print. This plan shows how to count the right things without collecting anyone's travel data, and how to get a clear yes or no on demand by mid-December for close to $0.

  1. Pick an analytics tool and make one account (recommended: GoatCounter, free, no cookies). Send Claude the one-line script it gives you. Claude adds it to every maker page with the funnel events below. Nothing is installed until you choose.

2b · Funnel events, and what never to collect

An event is a named counter: "someone clicked Download." It carries no flight list, no name, no email. These are the event names to use across all makers, so the numbers add up:

Funnel stepEvent nameFires when
Startstart-manual · start-sample · start-aiFirst flight added / sample loaded / AI import succeeds (once per visit)
Stylestyle-1 … style-5A style is picked (tells you which designs to print)
Finishdownload-story · watch-story · open-logStory PNG saved / animated story opened / log opened
Shareshare-instagram · share-facebook · share-threads · share-line · invite-friendsShare button tapped (counts intent; we can't see whether they posted)
Moneyprint-notify · print-etsy · tip"Notify me" tapped / Etsy link tapped / tip link tapped

How it will look in the code (Claude does this part). A small helper sends only the event name and silently does nothing if the analytics script is blocked:

// One helper for every maker. Sends a name only, never user data.
function track(name) {
  try {
    if (window.goatcounter && window.goatcounter.count) {
      window.goatcounter.count({ path: name, event: true });
    }
  } catch (e) { /* analytics must never break the maker */ }
}

document.getElementById('download').addEventListener('click', () => track('download-story'));

// Simple buttons can skip JS entirely:
// <button data-goatcounter-click="print-notify">Notify me about prints</button>

Never send to analytics, in event names, page URLs or anywhere else:

  • Flight details: airports, routes, dates, airlines, flight numbers, or the year someone typed
  • Names (the maker's "name" field), emails, or anything pasted into the AI import box
  • The Story picture or any generated image
  • Free-text input of any kind, even "just to debug"

The makers already keep everything in the visitor's browser. Analytics must keep that promise: counts of button names only. Share links get UTM tags (section 4) but never the person's data.

3 · Holiday demand test

Goal: by Dec 15, know whether strangers will pay for a print, spending under $10. Three cheap tests run side by side from Nov 10. Print-on-demand shipping for the US usually needs orders by about Dec 10–12 to arrive before Christmas, so treat Dec 10 as the last day for gift orders and say so on the listing. Assumption: check your print provider's published holiday cutoffs

A · "Notify me about prints"

A button after Download. It opens a short, honest panel: "Prints are coming: posters from about $24, small prints from about $12. Want to know when? This is a waitlist; nothing is charged."

Counts: print-notify taps (M6). Optional: a link to a separate email form with an unticked consent box (section 5).

Cost: $0.

B · One Etsy listing

"Your year in flights, personalized poster." One listing, two sizes: about $26 poster, $14 small print, made through an Etsy-connected print partner (e.g. Gelato or Printify). Buyer sends their flight list after ordering.

Counts: listing views, favorites, orders (Etsy Stats) and print-etsy taps from your makers.

Cost: $0.20 listing fee; about 10% of each sale in fees (6.5% transaction + ~3% + $0.25 payment processing in the US). Leave Etsy Offsite Ads off unless required.

C · Ask 10–15 friends

Ask about past behavior, not opinions of your idea:

  1. What did you last give as a gift under $30?
  2. Who do you know who flies or travels a lot?
  3. (Show a mockup.) Would you give this to them? Why or why not?
  4. What would you expect to pay for it?
  5. Want me to make one for you now? (Send the Etsy link.)

Counts: yeses to Q5 that actually pay. Kind words don't count.

UTM link rules

UTM tags are labels added to the end of a link so the analytics tool can tell which post sent a visitor. GoatCounter reads utm_source as the source; Plausible and Umami read all five tags. Rules: all lowercase, hyphens instead of spaces, never a person's name or data.

5 · Legal notes

This is a plain-language checklist, not legal advice. Before taking real payments at scale, a short consult (many law schools run free clinics for student founders) is worth it.

Privacy policy wording

Add a short /privacy.html linked in every maker's footer. Starting text:

Your flights, names and pictures stay in your browser. We never upload them.

We count visits and button taps with GoatCounter, a privacy-friendly analytics
service that uses no cookies and does not track you across sites. It records the
page, the referring site, browser type, screen size and country. Your IP address
is used briefly to estimate country and count unique visits, and is not stored.

If you join the prints waitlist, we keep your email only to tell you about prints,
send nothing else, and delete it on request. Every email has an unsubscribe link.
Our email provider is [name]. Contact: [email or contact form link].

We don't sell personal information. Last updated: [date].

CAN-SPAM checklist for any email list

  • Honest "From" name and subject line. No fake "Re:" or urgency.
  • Make clear when an email is promotional.
  • Include a valid postal address. A P.O. box or a registered private mailbox counts, so you don't need to publish your home address.
  • A working unsubscribe link in every email; honor it within 10 business days and never charge or ask for more data to unsubscribe.
  • You stay responsible if a helper or tool sends on your behalf.

Before selling prints

  • Remove airline logos from anything you sell. The free Story designs show airline badges. Logos are trademarks, and selling them on posters invites takedowns. Use airport codes and plain text instead on paid products.
  • On Etsy, disclose your print partner as a production partner and use real mockups of the printed product.
  • Keep the waitlist honest: it's a waitlist, nothing is charged, and you only email about prints.
  • Etsy collects and remits sales tax for US marketplace orders. Sales you take directly (friends via payment apps) are on you, so keep a simple sales log for taxes.
